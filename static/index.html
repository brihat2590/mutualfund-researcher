How to read this research

A window, not a single date

Each rolling observation measures the annualized return of a five-year holding period. The average, median and volatility describe how outcomes varied across entry dates.

A rating, not a recommendation

The score combines rolling performance, expenses, consistency, AUM and trailing returns. Open a fund's scorecard for the factor weights. Minimum SIP is shown for reference, not scored.

Data source: Dhan · Amounts in INR
